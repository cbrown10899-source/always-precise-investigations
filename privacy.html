Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies for visitors and carries no advertising or social-media trackers. The only cookie we set anywhere is a sign-in cookie on our private staff area, which the public site does not use.
  • We record which pages are visited, how long they are read and which contact buttons are used, so we can respond promptly to enquiries. Your browser keeps a random identifier so a return visit can be recognised — it is not a cookie and is never shared.
  • Nothing you type into the intake form is recorded unless you submit it.
  • Information you enter in the client intake form is sent to us so we can evaluate your matter. We treat it as confidential.
  • We do not sell information about you to anyone.

Information collected automatically

When someone visits this website we record a small amount of information about the visit: the pages viewed and roughly how long each one was actually being read (time a page sits unattended or in a background tab is not counted), the website that referred the visit (its name only, never the page you were on), whether the Call, Email, Contact or intake buttons were used, and an approximate city and region derived from the network connection — a guess about the connection, not where you are. This tells us when the site is being used so we can respond quickly to people who are trying to reach us.

To recognise a return visit, your browser keeps a random identifier in its own storage for this site. It is not a cookie, it identifies a browser rather than a person, it is never shared with anyone, and clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it.

If you open the client intake form we record that it was opened and that it was started — never anything you type into it. If you submit it, the visit is linked to the request number of your submission, so we can see how you found us; what you entered stays in the intake record described below.

What we do not do: we set no cookies on the public site, we do not build advertising profiles, we do not track visitors across other websites, we do not fingerprint your device, and we do not store your IP address or browser details. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal, nothing about the visit is recorded at all. Visit records older than 90 days are deleted automatically.

The client intake form

If you complete the intake form, you choose what to tell us — typically your contact details, the type of matter, information about the subject of the investigation, and your objective. We use it solely to evaluate whether we can help and to contact you about it.

What you enter is stored in our own case system, on infrastructure we control. It is reachable only by named members of our staff who sign in with their own account, and investigators can see only the cases assigned to them.

We also send ourselves an email alert so we know an enquiry has arrived. That alert travels through a third-party delivery service, so it deliberately contains only your case number, your name and how to reach you — never the subject of the investigation, what you are trying to establish, or your signature. Those never leave our own systems. We keep intake information only as long as needed for the matter and our record-keeping obligations.

Please do not send anything you would not want in an email. If a matter is especially sensitive, call us at (434) 907-0975 and we will take the details by phone instead.

Confidentiality

We treat client information as confidential and disclose it only as needed to perform the investigation you have engaged us for, to the client or their attorney, or where we are required to do so by law, court order, or the rules governing licensed private investigators in Virginia. We are licensed and regulated by the Virginia Department of Criminal Justice Services (DCJS #11-9159).

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email or call and we will respond promptly. We set no cookies for visitors and store no advertising identifiers. If you would rather your visits were not recorded at all, turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser; clearing this site’s data removes the visit identifier.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this site.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
