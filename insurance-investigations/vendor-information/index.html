Working With Us

Billing and conduct standards

No mileage or travel charges

Within the defined coverage area, travel time and mileage are not billed. A surveillance budget set against a reserve should match the invoice that arrives.

Rates confirmed in writing

The applicable rate and any estimated hours are confirmed before work begins. Submitting an assignment does not by itself constitute acceptance.

Authorization caps respected

Authorized hours are not exceeded without prior approval from the assigning contact. If the file needs more, you hear about it before it is spent.

Lawful, defensible method

Observation from places the public may be. No trespass, no recording where privacy is reasonably expected, no tracking devices, no pretext for financial or medical records, no contact with the claimant.

Negative results reported plainly

A day that produced nothing is reported as a day that produced nothing. A file that stretches once cannot be relied on anywhere.

Evidence handling

Time-stamped, unedited video and photographs with an intact chain of custody, delivered to the assigning party and its counsel only.

Testimony

The investigator who performed the work is available for deposition and hearing testimony as to what they personally observed.

Confidentiality

Claim information is kept confidential except as required by law, and work product is not released to anyone outside the assigning party and its counsel.
